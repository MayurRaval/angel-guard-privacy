Privacy Policy for Angel Guard

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Angel Guard (package mj.com.angelguard) handles information. Please read it before using the app.

The short version

Angel Guard has no account system and no login, and we never ask for your name, email or other contact details. The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, which collects some data — see "Advertising" below. If the game crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix it — see "Crash reports". And only if you choose "Allow" when the game asks, it shares anonymous gameplay statistics — see "Usage statistics (optional)". You can change that choice at any time in the game's Settings.

Information we collect

The app collects only:

  • Crash reports, through Google Firebase Crashlytics (below)
  • Usage statistics, through Google Firebase Analytics, and only if you allow it (below)

The app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information

Google AdMob, which serves the ads, does collect data in order to show them — described under "Advertising".

Crash reports

When the game crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics the next time it opens, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the technical details of the crash (where in the program it happened), the device model, Android version, app version, the screen and level you were on, and a random identifier for this installation of the app. It does not contain your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.

Usage statistics (optional)

The first time you open the game, it asks "Help improve Angel Guard?". Nothing is shared unless you choose "Allow". If you choose "No thanks", or have not answered, no usage statistics are sent at all, and the game works exactly the same.

If you allow it, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Firebase Analytics: for example which levels you start, finish, fail or leave and on which wave, which screens you visit, how long a session lasts, and which optional ads you choose to watch. Firebase also records basic device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate country derived from your IP address) and a random identifier for this installation. The game does not give Analytics your advertising ID, and the statistics are not used for advertising or shared with advertisers. We use them only to see where players get stuck or stop playing, so we can improve the game.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Turning it off stops all usage statistics and clears the Analytics data stored on your device. Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months. This processing is done by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — completed levels, stars, in-game currency and settings — to a file in the app's own private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

The game itself only needs internet access, which Google AdMob uses to request ads and Firebase uses to send crash reports and (if you allow it) usage statistics. Your game progress is never uploaded — it stays on your device.

To show those ads, the app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK, which adds these Android permissions:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to request ads and check whether you are online
  • AD_ID — to read your device's resettable advertising identifier
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features
  • READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE — basic, non-identifying device information such as network type and software version
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE — used by Android's background task scheduler, which the ads SDK relies on

The app does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising

Most ads in Angel Guard are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Occasionally, after some level wins, the game offers a short rewarded ad between levels; it shows a countdown first and you can skip it. There are no banner ads and no ads during a level.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device type, ad interactions, and diagnostic information about ad and app performance. This processing is Google's, under the Google Privacy & Terms. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline, you will still be able to watch rewarded ads, but they will be non-personalised. You can review or change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game, which reopens Google's consent form. You can also reset your advertising identifier or turn off ad personalisation entirely in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Other third-party services

Apart from Google AdMob and Google Firebase (Crashlytics, and Analytics only with your consent), no third-party services receive data about you.

Distribution is through Google Play, which collects its own data (such as installs and crash reports) under Google's Privacy Policy. That processing is Google's, not ours, and is outside our control.

Children's privacy

Angel Guard is suitable for general audiences but is not directed at children, and it is not part of the Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under any applicable age of digital consent. Ad content is limited by the maximum ad content rating configured for the app in Google AdMob.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We do not hold your name or contact details. Crash reports and usage statistics are linked only to a random installation identifier; to stop usage statistics and clear them from your device, turn off Settings → Share usage stats, and to ask us to delete data held in Firebase, email us at the address below. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the controls in Google My Ad Center and your device's advertising ID settings. Game progress lives only on your device and you can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data is collected — for example by adding cloud saves or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change. Please check back after updates.
