Privacy Policy for Angel Guard

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Angel Guard (package mj.com.angelguard) handles information. Please read it before using the app.

The short version

Angel Guard has no account system and no login, and we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers. The game does show optional rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for in-game bonuses. Those ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some data — see "Advertising" below.

Information we collect

We collect nothing directly. The app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information

Google AdMob, which serves the ads, does collect data in order to show them — described in the next section.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — completed levels, stars, in-game currency and settings — to a file in the app's own private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

The app declares the INTERNET permission, which Google AdMob uses to request the rewarded ads described above. Your game progress is never uploaded — it stays on your device. No other permissions are requested.

Advertising

Angel Guard shows rewarded video ads only — you are never shown an ad unless you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and ad interactions. This processing is Google's, under the Google Privacy & Terms. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline, you will still be able to watch rewarded ads, but they will be non-personalised. You can review or change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game, which reopens Google's consent form. You can also reset your advertising identifier or turn off ad personalisation entirely in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Other third-party services

There are no analytics SDKs and no other third-party services that receive data about you.

Distribution is through Google Play, which collects its own data (such as installs and crash reports) under Google's Privacy Policy. That processing is Google's, not ours, and is outside our control.

Children's privacy

Angel Guard is suitable for general audiences but is not directed at children, and it is not part of the Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under any applicable age of digital consent. Ad content is limited by the maximum ad content rating configured for the app in Google AdMob.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the controls in Google My Ad Center and your device's advertising ID settings. Game progress lives only on your device and you can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces anything that collects data — such as advertising, analytics, cloud saves or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change. Please check back after updates.
