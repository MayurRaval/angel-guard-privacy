Privacy Policy for Angel Guard

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Angel Guard (package com.angelguard.game) handles information. Please read it before using the app.

The short version

Angel Guard does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The game has no account system, no login, and does not send any information off your device.

Information we collect

None. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Photos, contacts, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — completed levels, stars, in-game currency and settings — to a file in the app's own private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Permissions

The app declares the INTERNET permission, which Android requires apps to list in advance. The current version does not transmit any data over the network. No other permissions are requested.

Third-party services

This version of Angel Guard contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party services that receive data about you.

Distribution is through Google Play, which collects its own data (such as installs and crash reports) under Google's Privacy Policy. That processing is Google's, not ours, and is outside our control.

Children's privacy

Angel Guard is suitable for general audiences. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or under any applicable age of digital consent — because we collect none at all.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. Game progress lives only on your device and you can remove it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces anything that collects data — such as advertising, analytics, cloud saves or in-app purchases — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change. Please check back after updates.
