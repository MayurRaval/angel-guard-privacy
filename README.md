# Angel Guard — Privacy Policy

Public hosting for the privacy policy of **Angel Guard**
(`com.angelguard.game`), as required by Google Play and the App Store.

Live page: https://mayurraval.github.io/angel-guard-privacy/

The policy states that the app collects no data. **If a future release adds
advertising, analytics, cloud saves or real in-app purchases, update
`index.html` (and the Play Data safety form) before that build ships.**
